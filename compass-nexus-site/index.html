One connected chain

From the employee's booking to your payment, one unbroken record

Each stage writes to the same trip. Nothing is re-keyed between operations and billing, so any figure on an invoice leads back to the trip it came from.

  1. Book

    A roster entry or customer request becomes a trip with its own Trip ID.

  2. Assign

    Vehicle and driver matched on availability, valid documents and site.

  3. Operate

    Live status, ETA and GPS route from pickup to drop.

  4. Verify

    Eleven checks and customer confirmation, then the trip is locked.

  5. Reconcile

    Booking, roster, GPS and trip sheet compared. Exceptions are flagged.

  6. Approve

    A person approves the billing record before anything is issued.

  7. Invoice

    Tax invoice with the full supporting package for the period.

  8. Get paid

    Payments tracked against each invoice and reported monthly.

Gold stages are human checkpoints. A trip cannot move past them automatically. Driving partners are paid from the same verified trips: see partner payouts.

Driving partner payouts

After every verified trip, the driving partner is paid on record

Payouts come from the same locked trips the customer is billed for. Each driving partner sees what they earned, trip by trip, before Finance releases the payment.

  1. Trip locked

    Only verified, locked trips count toward a payout.

  2. Earnings calculated

    At the rate in the partner agreement: per trip, per KM or per shift.

  3. Statement to the partner

    The driver app shows every trip and amount. The partner can query any line.

  4. Finance approves

    A person approves each payout run. Nothing is released automatically.

  5. Paid and linked

    Bank transfer or UPI. The payment reference is saved against every trip it covers.

  • Disputed or unverified trips are held, never dropped
  • Deductions such as advances are listed line by line with a reason
  • Customer billing and partner payouts reconcile per period for Finance

Questions

What transport and finance teams usually ask

Do you contract directly or through a partner?

Both. IBS Software and Envestnet are direct accounts, where we hold the agreement and manage operations, billing and reporting ourselves. We also run services as a partner to MLL Mobility for Allianz, EY, GMC, HCL, Vizhinjam Port and Infoblox. The platform keeps direct and sub-vendor accounts separate in every record and report.

Can our finance team check an invoice against the trips?

Yes. Each invoice links to its billing period, the trip ledger and every verified trip in it. The package includes the detailed trip sheet, KM summary, rate calculation and tax calculation so each line can be checked.

What happens if a trip record needs correcting after it is locked?

The correction is recorded as a change, never an overwrite. The audit trail keeps the old value, the new value, who changed it, when, and the reason.

How are driving partners paid?

From verified trips only. At the end of each payout period the partner gets a statement in the driver app listing every trip, the agreed rate and any deductions. Finance approves the run, the payment goes by bank transfer or UPI, and the reference is stored against each trip. Disputed trips are held until they are resolved.

Does the AI issue invoices by itself?

No. It prepares draft invoices, reconciliations and exception reports. A person reviews and approves before any invoice is issued, and possible problems are flagged for review rather than treated as wrongdoing.

Who can see driver and employee personal details?

Only the roles that need them. Driver licence, verification and medical records are restricted, and customer admins see driver details only where the contract permits.

Where do you operate?

We are based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, and serve IT companies, corporate campuses, industrial organisations and port operations.
